(a) The Guidelines shall be adopted by the Secretary for Resources. The Secretary shall make a finding that each class of projects given a categorical exemption will not have a significant effect on the environment. (b) The Secretary may issue amendments to these Guidelines. (c) The Secretary shall certify state environmental regulatory programs which meet the standards for certification in Section 21080.5, Public Resources Code. (d) The Secretary shall receive and file notices required by certified state environmental regulatory programs.
